Privacy Policy

Mecha Paws · Effective October 22, 2026 · Last updated October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Mecha Paws (the “Game”, bundle id and package name com.minhnguyen.mechapaws) collects and how it is used. The Game is made by Minh Nguyen (“we”, “us”). You do not create an account to play, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number inside the Game.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (heroes, cards, currencies, chapters, settings) is saved in a file on your device. It is not sent to any server we operate.

2. Cloud save in your platform account

So you keep your progress after reinstalling the Game or changing phones, the Game signs in to your platform's game service and keeps a copy of the save file there: Google Play Games Services (Saved Games) on Android, and Apple Game Center saved games (stored in your iCloud account) on iOS. The copy contains only the game data described above and is stored in your own Google or Apple account under their privacy policies. You can decline the sign-in and keep playing; the save then stays only on your device. Android may also include the save in your device backup if you enabled it.

3. Information collected by third-party services

The Game uses the services below. They may collect technical data such as device model, operating system version, language, approximate location derived from the IP address, and pseudonymous identifiers:

  • Advertising (Google AdMob): shows rewarded videos (always your choice) and banners in some game modes. AdMob may use the Google Advertising ID (Android) or, only if you allow it in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, the IDFA (iOS). Where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states), a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform appears first, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy settings. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.
  • In-app purchases (RevenueCat, Google Play Billing, Apple StoreKit): purchases are processed by Google or Apple. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user id, the products you bought and store receipts, so purchases can be verified and restored. We never see or store your card or bank details.
  • Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase), if enabled in your version: anonymous gameplay events (for example a chapter cleared, an ad watched, a purchase made), device and app information, and crash reports. They are only collected after the consent step above allows it, and are never used for ad personalization.

4. Notifications and review prompts

If you allow notifications, reminders (for example “your patrol sack is full”) are scheduled locally on your device; nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in Settings → Reminders or in your device settings. The Game may show the standard Apple or Google rating sheet; we do not receive your rating or account details from it, and no reward is ever given for rating.

5. How we use information

To run the Game and keep your progress, restore your purchases, show ads and measure their performance, prevent fraud, fix crashes and improve the Game's balance. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

Consent for personalized advertising and analytics (you can withdraw it in Settings → Privacy settings); performance of a contract for purchases and cloud save; legitimate interests for security, fraud prevention and crash diagnostics.

7. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (16 in some EU countries). If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices and rights

You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings, decline ATT on iOS, change consent in the Game, and delete all game data by uninstalling the Game (and deleting the cloud copy in Google Play Games or Game Center / iCloud settings). Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data; because we do not keep an account database, most requests are handled by the services listed above, but we will help: write to us at the address below.
