Support

Help for Mecha Paws on iPhone and Android

Write to minhnguyen.de.vn@gmail.com. Please include your phone model, the game version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and what happened. We usually answer within two working days.

My progress is gone after reinstalling

Sign in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone) with the same account you used before, open the Game and wait a minute: the cloud copy is restored automatically. You can check it in Settings → Cloud Save and press Sync now.

A purchase did not arrive / I changed phones

Open Settings → Restore Purchases. One-time purchases (Remove Ads, perks, Hero Vault squads, the Lifetime Paw Pass) come back on the same store account. Gems and other consumables are added when bought and are kept in your save and its cloud copy.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or Google Play's order history for Android.

Ads and privacy

Rewarded ads are always optional. In the EEA and the UK you can change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy settings. Read the Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the Game, then remove its saved game in Google Play Games settings or in iCloud settings (Game Center saved games). Because the Game has no account on our side, there is nothing else to delete; if you want us to confirm anything, write to us.
